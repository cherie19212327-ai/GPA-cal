FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How is GPA calculated?
GPA is calculated using a weighted average formula. Each course grade is converted to grade points (A=4.0, B=3.0, etc.), then multiplied by the course's credit hours to give quality points. Your GPA is the total quality points divided by total credit hours attempted. GradeIQ handles all this computation automatically.
What grading scale does GradeIQ use?
GradeIQ uses the standard US 4.0 grading scale: A+/A = 4.0, A- = 3.7, B+ = 3.3, B = 3.0, B- = 2.7, and so on down to F = 0.0. This is the most widely used scale across American universities. Custom scale support is also planned for future updates.
Can I calculate GPA for multiple semesters?
Yes! GradeIQ fully supports multi-semester GPA calculation. You can enter your previous cumulative GPA and total credit hours completed, and GradeIQ will automatically calculate your updated cumulative GPA alongside your current semester GPA — giving you a complete academic picture.
Is my academic data safe?
Absolutely. All GPA calculations are performed entirely in your browser using JavaScript. No data is sent to any server, stored in any database, or shared with any third party. Your academic information stays completely private on your own device.
Is GradeIQ free to use?
Yes, GradeIQ is completely free to use — no account required, no hidden fees, no premium tier. Every feature is available to every student at no cost. We believe every student deserves access to great academic tools regardless of their budget.
